# Update 2: Living Village

User approved execution after 20 design answers. Use free `opencode/space-bunny-free` for implementation. Controller remains planner, reviewer and final verifier. No paid fallback, source Blender changes, commits, pushes or deployment.

## Requirements

Clash-inspired layout/styling with an original dark-medieval identity. Stacked top-right resources, categorized building cards with real thumbnails, compact contextual bottom actions. Defenders act intelligently: melee intercepts threats, archers keep range and retreat safely; manual orders still override.

Outdoor workers use job-specific facing targets and separate reachable work positions, smooth turns, correct existing animations and no work loops while inactive. All friendly villagers wear slow, connected, soft-edged trails through actual movement. Existing fixed cross paths are removed. Unused trails regrow during active time, wear survives saves, and sensible routes prefer existing paths without excessive detours.

New Stone resource and quarry precede stone-paving research. Minimal research chain: Stoneworking unlocks quarry; Road Masonry unlocks paving. Insight is generated by a living finished manor, one active research job, paid once, saved, paused during alarms/focus loss. Quarry uses miner role and the existing gather/delivery/cap rules. Paving is confirmed per existing dirt-path tile, costs Stone, permanent, friendly movement +15%, no passive production bonus and no enemy speed boost.

Selected extras: connected walls/gates, construction/upgrade scaffolding and progress, incoming raid-side/danger indicators, restrained ground/work/smoke/lighting detail. Not selected: squad-control rail, bulk-road tools, cargo markers, full campaign/tech tree/multiplayer.

## Proposed Starting Balance

Trail wear is distance-based: faint ~25 equivalent crossings, established ~150. Idle/in-place rotations make no wear. Unused dirt starts decaying after 10 active quiet minutes; roughly an hour of disuse removes a fully worn path. No closed-game decay. Stone floors: central base800, manor400, storehouse1600 per tier multiplier. Quarry size2, Wood80/Gold40, build6s, Stone1/s, reserve500, cap2, two miners. Stoneworking: village level2, Insight20/Wood40/Gold20,60s; Road Masonry: prerequisite Stoneworking,Insight30/Stone40,90s. Manor insight6/min, cap100. Paving Stone8 per2m logical tile; only visibly established dirt, no building overlap, live validation, all-or-nothing spend.
