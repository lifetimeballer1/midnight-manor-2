Ambience and event sound credits
================================

day.ogg, night.ogg, owl.ogg, water.ogg, fanfare.ogg, war_drums.ogg
  Synthesised from code for Midnight Manor by scripts/audio/make_soundscape.py (no recorded samples).
  Re-run that script to rebuild them; the seed is fixed.

The soundscape also plays these existing Kenney CC0 sounds from audio/combat/ for work near the camera:
  impactMetal_*.ogg (forges), chop.ogg (lumber camps), creak*.ogg (mills).
